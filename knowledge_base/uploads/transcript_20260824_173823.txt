- **Introduction**: 
  - The scenario is set in a country club where a wedding party was supposed to be taking place.
  
- **Initial Discovery**:
  - Adriana remembered leaving her night cash drop behind earlier.
  - They attempted to return it, but when they arrived at the location, about an hour had passed, making the time 11:45.

- **Observations Upon Arrival**:
  - The front law (parking area) was full and busy earlier with people attending the wedding.
  - Now, it is completely empty, indicating no ongoing event or activity.
  
  - A car was still parked in the front area despite a party being supposedly taking place inside.

- **Suspicions**:
  - The silence and absence of noise coming from the building (music, voices) raised suspicions among Adriana and Laura.
  
  - They noticed something odd: There is no wedding music or commotion coming from inside the building, only a muffled sound drifting outwards.
  
- **Investigation Inside Building**:
  - Both women attempted to enter through the doors of the building but found them all locked.
  
  - Once inside, they discovered that the interior was completely dark.

- **Unexpected Sound Discovery**:
  - It became clear that there is an ongoing event happening within the building, despite the outside being silent and empty.
  - The sound heard from the side of the building was not typical wedding music but something else. It did not match the expected sounds for a modern dance party or a band.

- **Conclusion**:
  - There are two contradictory aspects: an apparently abandoned area with locked doors, indicating no event taking place inside; and evidence suggesting there is indeed an ongoing event within.
  
  - This situation has created uncertainty and fear among Adriana and Laura.
